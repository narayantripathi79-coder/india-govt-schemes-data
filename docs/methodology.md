## Methodology

This project focuses on structuring publicly available information about
Indian government schemes into machine-readable formats.

### Data Collection
Data is collected only from official and publicly accessible government portals.

### Processing
Raw information is converted into a standardized schema that captures key
attributes such as eligibility, benefits, beneficiary type, and funding source.

### Output
The final structured datasets are exported in CSV and JSON formats for reuse
in analysis, applications, and AI systems.

No personal or sensitive data is collected.
